## 1. Executive Summary & Evidence-Driven Taxonomy

The **Molexplorer Scientific Benchmark & Validation Framework (P4.5)** establishes an authoritative, quantitative verification harness for biophysical algorithms, coordinate parsing, selection algebra, spatial geometry, non-covalent interactions, and molecular state persistence. 

In accordance with strict computational biophysics evaluation standards:
1. **Pre-execution status** was maintained as `NOT YET VALIDATED / PROPOSED` until live execution against independent references.
2. **Classification** is evidence-driven across a 6-tier status taxonomy.
3. **Internal vs External Independence**: Internal comparisons (e.g. legacy `SelectionParser` vs canonical evaluator) are strictly classified as `SOFTWARE VERIFIED`. External literature, crystallographic records, and analytical oracles warrant `SCIENTIFICALLY VALIDATED` or `GEOMETRICALLY / RULE-BASED VALIDATED`.
4. **Tolerance Provenance**: Every tolerance specifies numerical magnitude, unit, tolerance type (`Numerical Precision`, `Reference-Data Uncertainty`, or `Acceptance Threshold`), and scientific justification without retroactive widening.
5. **Topology Split**: Explicit PDB `CONECT` preservation (H1) is benchmarked against exact discrete edge matching, while distance-based covalent perception (H2) is benchmarked as a heuristic inference.

## 6. Scientific Limitations & Boundaries

The following scientific boundary conditions and limitations are formally recorded as domain reference metadata:
1. **DSSP Modeling**: The Kabsch-Sander algorithm requires explicit amide hydrogen positions. In crystal structures where experimental hydrogens are not present, modeled geometric positions are used; local electrostatic energy thresholds ($E < -0.5\text{ kcal/mol}$) are sensitive to terminal backbone conformations.
2. **Backbone Torsions**: Ramachandran contour classifications (Lovell et al. 2003) reflect empirical frequency statistics of high-resolution structures, rather than absolute thermodynamic free energy minima.
3. **Electrostatic Dipoles**: Dipole calculations employ AMBER ff99 fixed partial charge models and mass-weighted center of mass origins. They do not simulate solvent screening continuum effects ($\epsilon_r = 78.4$) or electronic polarizability ($\alpha$).
4. **Non-Covalent Contacts**: Distance/angle contact criteria determine geometric proximity and sterically plausible interactions; they do not calculate binding enthalpy ($\Delta H$) or free energy ($\Delta G$).
5. **Covalent Perception**: Alvarez radius inference operates on standard covalent radii heuristics ($+0.45\text{ \AA}$ tolerance). Non-standard coordination complexes (e.g. organometallic centers) require explicit CONECT records.
